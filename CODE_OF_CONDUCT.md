# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AccessPath is built around inclusive access and respectful collaboration. We want participation to be welcoming to people of every background, identity, experience level, and ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome different perspectives and lived experiences.
- Use inclusive language and focus feedback on ideas and code.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from feedback.
- Prioritize accessibility, privacy, safety, and dignity in project decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or other inappropriate conduct.
- Knowingly sharing fabricated accessibility or safety claims.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-related interactions.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through the contact options on the GitHub profile. Include relevant context and links where possible. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. The goal is to protect a safe, respectful, and constructive project community.
